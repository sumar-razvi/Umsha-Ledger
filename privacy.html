UMSHA Ledger: privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

UMSHA Ledger is the internal accounting and operations system of UMSHA and its ready-to-wear brand Mirae. It is used only by people the business has added as users.

What we receive when you sign in

When you sign in with Google, Google tells the Ledger your name and email address. We use them only to check that you are one of our users, to show which screens your role allows, and to record who entered each sale, order or payment. We do not receive your Google password and do not read your Gmail, Drive or any other Google data.

Business records

The records you enter (sales, orders, customers, payments, stock) are stored in the business's own Google Sheet and Google Drive. They are used to run the business and keep its accounts, and are seen only by its staff and its accountant.

Sharing

We do not sell or share this information with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Questions or removal

To ask what is held about you, or to have your access removed, contact the business owner. Accounting records must be kept by law, so entries already made are kept even after access ends.
