# UMSHA Ledger: guide for store and studio staff

This guide is for the people who use the Ledger every day at the Mirae store and in the UMSHA studio. You do not need to know accounting. The system does the accounting for you when you record what happened: a sale, an advance, karigar work, a delivery.

Finance and the Owners have their own screens (reports, banking, month close). They are not covered here.

---

## 1. Getting started

**Signing in.** Open the Ledger link on your phone or computer and tap **Sign in with Google**. Use the Google account the Owner added for you. If it says your account has no access, ask the Owner.

**What you see.** You only see the screens for your job:

| You are | Your menu |
|---|---|
| Store staff | New sale · Returns · Today · Cash-up · Petty expense |
| Studio staff | Orders · Customers · Inventory · Batches · Karigars |

**Typing amounts.** Type rupees the normal way: `1250`, `1,250.50`, `125k` (125,000) or `1.25L` (1 lakh 25 thousand). The amount appears under the box as rupees so you can check it.

**The green "Saved" sign (top of the screen).**

- **Saved**: everything you entered has reached the system.
- **Saving…**: it is being sent. Wait a moment.
- **Offline**: the internet dropped. **Do not enter it again.** The Ledger keeps what you entered on the phone and sends it by itself when the connection comes back. It will never be recorded twice.

**Mistakes.** Nothing can be deleted, on purpose: every rupee must be traceable. If you entered something wrong, tell Finance. They will cancel it with a correcting entry, and you enter it again correctly.

## 2. Store staff (Mirae)

### Make a sale: **New sale**

1. Scan the barcode or type part of the SKU name or code, and tap the item. Tap again (or use **+**) for more than one piece.
2. Optional: a discount on one line, or **Discount on whole sale**.
3. Optional: **Customer mobile** if they want the receipt on WhatsApp (and their name if they are new).
4. Under **Payment**, tap **Cash**, **Card**, **Bank / Raast** or **Wallet**. For a split payment (some cash, some card), enter each amount; the total must match.
5. Tap **Complete sale**. The receipt opens: print it, or tap **WhatsApp** to send it.

Prices already include sales tax. The receipt shows the tax separately.

If the screen says **Only … in stock**, do not ring up a different item instead. Tell the studio or Finance: the stock count is probably wrong.

### Returns and exchanges: **Returns**

1. Find the original sale by its receipt number, the customer's mobile or the date. Tap **Find**.
2. Tap the pieces coming back (**Return one more** / **Return one less**). Tick **Back on the shelf** if the piece can be sold again; leave it unticked if it is damaged.
3. Either **Refund** the customer (choose how: cash, card, bank), or tap **Exchange for something else instead** and add the new items. If the new items cost more, the customer pays the difference; if less, you refund the difference.
4. Tap **Confirm**. A return receipt opens.

A return always uses the original sale's price, not today's price.

### Your day: **Today**

Shows today's sales, returns, number of transactions and how customers paid. Use it to check the day before cash-up. Tap **Dashboard** for the week and month, average sale and returns (your store only).

### End of the day: **Cash-up**

1. Count the cash in the drawer.
2. The screen shows **Cash that should be in the drawer**. Type what you counted in **Cash counted**, add a note if they differ, and tap **Submit cash-up**.
3. If cash is short or over, the difference is recorded. That is fine: write a note explaining it. Do not change the count to make it match.

Cash-up can be done once per day. Card and wallet money goes to the bank by itself; Finance records it when it arrives.

### Small store expenses: **Petty expense**

For small things paid from the store's petty cash (tea, cleaning, courier):

1. **What kind of expense**, **Amount**, **Date**, a short **Note**.
2. Take a **Photo of the receipt** (keep the paper receipt too).
3. **Save**.

Store staff can only pay from petty cash. Anything else goes to Finance.

## 3. Studio staff (UMSHA)

### Book an order: **Orders → New order**

1. **Customer**: type a name or mobile number. If they are new, tap **+ New customer** and enter their name and mobile (the mobile is used for WhatsApp receipts).
2. Order title (e.g. "Bridal lehenga, red") and **Promised delivery** date.
3. Add the pieces and their prices (**Quoted (before tax)**). The screen shows the sales tax and the **Total for customer**.
4. If the customer pays something now, enter the **First advance** and where it was received (studio cash, bank…).
5. Tap **Book order**. The advance receipt opens: print it or send it on WhatsApp.

An advance is the customer's money that we hold until delivery. It is not a sale yet.

### Work on an order (the order page)

Open the order from **Orders**. The buttons you will use:

| Button | When |
|---|---|
| **Receive advance** | The customer pays more before delivery. |
| **Start production** | Work has begun. |
| **Issue materials** | Fabric or trims leave the store room for this order. Choose the item and quantity. |
| **Return materials** | Unused fabric or trims come back to the store room. |
| **Log karigar work** | A karigar did work on this order. Choose the karigar, the kind of work, pieces and rate per piece. This creates the bill to pay them. |
| **Mark ready** | The outfit is finished and waiting for the customer. |
| **Deliver** | The customer takes the order. Check the invoice and tap **Confirm delivery**. The advances are used up and the balance due is shown. |
| **Receive balance** | The customer pays what is left after delivery. |
| **WhatsApp** | Send the customer a message about the receipt or balance. |
| **Cancel order** | Only if the customer cancels. Ask the Owner first: what is refunded and what is kept is a business decision. |

The **Cost sheet** on the order page shows what the order has cost so far (materials and karigar work). It updates as you issue materials and log work.

### Customers

Find any customer by name or mobile to see their orders, payments and what they owe.

### Karigars

**Log karigar work** here too (choose the order or batch). The list shows each karigar's work and what they are owed. Payments to karigars are made by Finance.

### Inventory

- **New item**: a new fabric, trim or Mirae SKU. Set the unit (metres, grams, pieces) and, for things that run out, a **Reorder level** (the dashboard warns when stock falls below it).
- Tap an item to see every movement: bought, issued to an order, returned, sold.
- **Stock count**: count what is on the shelf and enter it. The system shows the difference before you post it. Count honestly; do not adjust numbers to match.

### Batches (Mirae production)

1. **Batches → New production batch**: name, the SKUs it will make and planned pieces.
2. While it is being made: **Issue materials** and **Log karigar work** against the batch.
3. **Complete batch**: enter the pieces actually made of each SKU. They move into finished stock for the store, each carrying its share of the batch cost.

### Dashboard

From **Orders**, tap **Dashboard**: the order book, orders past their promised date, average order value and margin, materials and karigar spend.

## 4. Questions

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Your sign-in could not be verified" | Sign out and in again. If it continues, tell the Owner. |
| "The system is busy" | Someone else is saving. The Ledger tries again by itself; wait. |
| The sign says **Offline** for a long time | Check the internet. Do not re-enter anything. When the connection is back it changes to **Saved**. |
| I entered the wrong amount | Tell Finance with the receipt or order number. |
| I can't see a screen a colleague has | Screens depend on your role. Ask the Owner if you need it. |
| A month is "closed" | Finance has closed that month. Entries for it are refused; ask Finance. |

**Words used in the Ledger**

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Advance | Money a customer pays before delivery. We hold it until the order is delivered. |
| Karigar | An artisan we pay per piece (embroidery, stitching, adda work). |
| Issue | Taking fabric or trims from the store room for an order or batch. |
| SKU | One Mirae design in one size. |
| Cash-up | Counting the store's cash at the end of the day. |
| Receipt number | `SALE-2026-00042` style numbers on every receipt. Use them to find a sale. |
